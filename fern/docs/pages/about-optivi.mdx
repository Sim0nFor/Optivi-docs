## What is Optivi

Optivi is a broker platform designed to simplify access to crypto option markets through a clean and intuitive user interface. Built on top of the Derive Protocol infrastructure, it allows users to access the most liquid markets, with all trades settled fully on-chain through a self-custodial derivatives protocol.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Self-custodial" icon="fa-duotone fa-shield-check">
    Your funds sit in a Derive subaccount that you own. Optivi never takes custody of them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settled on-chain" icon="fa-duotone fa-link">
    Every trade is settled through the Derive Protocol.
  </Card>
  <Card title="BTC, ETH, SOL and HYPE" icon="fa-duotone fa-coins">
    Trade options on four markets from one interface.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Defined risk" icon="fa-duotone fa-scale-balanced">
    When you buy an option, the most you can lose is the premium you paid. There are no liquidations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What assets can I trade?">
    Optivi lets you trade options on \$BTC, \$ETH, \$SOL and \$HYPE.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How are orders executed and settled? Is custody self-managed?">
    Trading is self-custodial. Orders are executed as signed messages; contracts verify maker/taker agreement on legs/prices before settlement to the Protocol.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which wallets can I use?">
    You can sign in by connecting a supported EVM wallet. Login is handled by Privy. See [Onboarding](/optivi-protocol/onboarding) for details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Derive account?">
    No separate sign-up is needed. When you make your first deposit, Optivi creates a dedicated subaccount on Derive for you. It will also be visible on your [Derive dashboard](https://app.derive.xyz/subaccounts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How can I contact support?">
    You can reach us on Telegram: [@optivixyz](https://t.me/optivixyz) or via email: [contact@optivi.xyz](mailto:contact@optivi.xyz)
  </Accordion>
